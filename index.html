Hey, I'm OVon.

I am a 21-year-old content creator and musician living in Austria. I started streaming in February 2025 and quickly fell in love with creating content and connecting with people online. Unfortunately, due to internet issues, I had to put streaming on hold for now. Even though I'm not currently streaming, I plan to return when the situation improves.

Alongside streaming, I began making music and released my first album, "Off Da Drank," on Spotify in 2025. Music has become a huge passion of mine, and I'm always looking to improve and create new projects. I also run a YouTube channel where I hope to upload more content in the future. Whether it's gaming, music, or other creative ideas, I want to build a place where people can enjoy what I create and follow my journey. None of this would be possible without the support of my friends. They help me with editing and music production, and I'm incredibly grateful for everything they do. Their support motivates me to keep pushing forward and improving every day.

Thanks for stopping by and being part of my journey. More content is coming soon!
